Privacy Policy

Vaidya Ashwani Live Call Translator · Last updated: 25 November 2026 (v1.1)

1. Summary

Vaidya Ashwani Live Call Translator ("the App") lets you make translated video and voice calls and translate phone/WhatsApp conversations played on speaker. We do not run user accounts, we do not sell data, and the App shows no ads. Your call history, PDFs and recordings stay on your device.

2. Data the App uses

  • Name, phone number and language that you enter. They are stored only on your device. Your phone number is used as your Call ID so that other App users can call you. When you are online, this ID is sent to the call signalling server.
  • Microphone audio is used for calls and for speech recognition. Speech recognition is done by your device's speech service (Google) or your browser.
  • Camera video is used only during video calls that you start or accept.
  • Notifications: while a call is active, the App shows an ongoing notification and runs a foreground service so that the microphone (and the camera in video calls) keeps working when the screen is locked. The service stops when the call ends.
  • Patient records (Admin only): an admin can save patient name, mobile number, address, who assigned the patient and notes, and link call recordings to the patient. These records are visible only after admin login, are stored only on the admin's device and are encrypted with AES-256 using a key derived from the admin password. They are never uploaded by the App.
  • Join by link: a person invited by link can join from a web browser without installing the App. They enter only a display name and a language. Nothing about them is stored on any server.
  • Recognised text of what is said during a call is sent to a translation service (Google Translate; MyMemory as fallback) to translate it into the listener's language. If your device has no voice for a language, that translated text is sent to Google's text-to-speech service to be read aloud.
  • Call recordings, transcripts and PDFs are created and stored only on your device. They leave your device only if you choose to share or export them.

3. How calls work

Calls are made directly between the two devices (peer-to-peer WebRTC) and are encrypted in transit (DTLS-SRTP). A signalling server (PeerJS) is used only to connect the two devices. If a direct connection is not possible, a relay (TURN) server may carry the encrypted media.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Translate / Google Text-to-Speech – policies.google.com/privacy
  • Android / Google speech recognition – policies.google.com/privacy
  • MyMemory translation (fallback) – mymemory.translated.net
  • PeerJS signalling server – peerjs.com

5. Recording consent

The App can record calls. When recording is on, the other person is shown a notice that the call is being recorded. You are still responsible for telling the other person and getting their consent before recording, as required by the laws where you live. You can turn recording off in Settings.

6. Data retention and deletion

Everything is stored on your device. To delete a single call, delete it in the History tab. To delete all App data, uninstall the App or clear its storage. We hold no copy of your data on our servers.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer through the Google Play Store listing of the App.
