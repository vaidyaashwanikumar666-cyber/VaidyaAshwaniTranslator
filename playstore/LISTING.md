## Full description (max 4000)
Vaidya Ashwani Live Call Translator lets you talk to anyone in your own language.

Speak in Hindi, and the other person hears you in Tamil, Bengali, English, Arabic or any other language. When they reply in their language, you hear it in Hindi. It works live, during the call.

★ THE OTHER PERSON DOESN'T NEED THE APP
Send a link on WhatsApp or SMS. The other person opens it in their phone's browser, picks their language and joins your call. They don't need to install anything or sign up.

★ LIVE TRANSLATED CALLS
• Video Call and Voice Call with live translation and on-screen subtitles
• Your mobile number is your Call ID, so there is no login and no account
• Translation is spoken aloud in the listener's language
• Can't speak? Type during the call and it is translated instantly
• The call keeps running even when your screen is locked

★ WHATSAPP & PHONE CALLS – SPEAKER TRANSLATOR
Put any WhatsApp or SIM call on speaker. The app listens to both sides, translates and speaks the translation, taking turns automatically.

★ EVERY CALL RECORDED
• A complete PDF of each conversation, showing time, who spoke, the original words and the translation
• A voice recording of each call, and the other person is told that the call is being recorded
• Call History lets you download a PDF or recording at any time

★ ALL INDIAN LANGUAGES
All 22 languages of the 8th Schedule: Assamese, Bengali, Bodo, Dogri, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Kashmiri, Konkani, Maithili, Malayalam, Manipuri, Marathi, Nepali, Odia, Punjabi, Sanskrit, Santali, Sindhi, Tamil, Telugu and Urdu.
Also: Bhojpuri, Awadhi, Marwadi, Tulu, Mizo, Khasi, Chhattisgarhi and English (India).

★ WORLD LANGUAGES
More than 120 languages, including English, Arabic, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Italian, Turkish, Persian, Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese and Swahili.

★ PRIVATE
Calls go directly between the two phones and are encrypted. Your history, PDFs and recordings are stored only on your phone. There are no ads and no account.

Perfect for doctors and patients, families abroad, business calls, travel and customer support.

Note: An internet connection is required. For WhatsApp or SIM calls, use Speaker Translator mode, because other apps' calls cannot be translated directly.
